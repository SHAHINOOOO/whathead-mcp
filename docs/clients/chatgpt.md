# Connect Whathead to ChatGPT

Available on ChatGPT **Pro, Business, and Enterprise** plans that support custom MCP
connectors.

1. Open **Settings → Connectors**.
2. Click **Add** → **Custom connector** (MCP).
3. Enter:
   - **Name:** Whathead
   - **URL:** `https://whathead.com/mcp`
4. Authorize with OAuth.
5. In a chat, enable the Whathead connector and ask it to work.

**Verify:**

> "Using Whathead, list my connected ad accounts and this week's spend."

> **Note:** Custom MCP connector availability depends on your ChatGPT plan and workspace
> admin settings.
